The system in one view

The product is a daily work assistant and an evidence-supported evaluation system. It reduces employee effort, connects work context, and preserves human authority over every official decision.

Implemented Human gate External gate Retained route

Daily work, simplified

Home summarizes multiple Projects. Work prioritizes decisions, Today, and Overdue. The assistant prepares one useful next step at a time.

Projects with real context

A Project connects its approved document, Workstreams, Tasks, sources, Updates, Evidence, Research, Progress Contract, Timeline, and human decisions.

Fair evaluation, human-decided

Source-supported facts help the employee and manager assess the same rubric. The manager makes the final judgment; AI never recommends a rating.

✓

The operating principle

AI prepares, connects, explains, and drafts. The employee or authorized owner edits, accepts, rejects, or defers. Official records change only through an existing protected command and the required human confirmation.

What the assistant removes

  • Repeated classification and copying between tools.
  • Searching manually for the related Project or Task when confidence is high.
  • Starting every Update, Evidence description, or check-in from a blank page.

What remains employee-controlled

  • Editing, rejecting, deferring, and confirming every official draft.
  • Confirming Evidence and personal contribution attribution.
  • Selecting a self-assessment rating before AI may help with editable wording.

6

Project progress—not employee performance

A visible percentage is allowed only when an active, versioned Progress Contract defines how approved milestone or KPI measurements produce it. Missing approved information remains visibly missing.

Contract + approved measurement
1

Approved Project Document

Purpose, deliverables, acceptance conditions, and source context.

2

AI-prepared proposal

Milestones, operational KPIs, baseline, target, unit, direction, required Evidence, and calculation rule.

3

Human activation

Authorized owner reviews, revises, saves, and activates a versioned contract.

4

Measurable change

Approved Evidence satisfies a rule, or an authorized human confirms the measurement with reason.

5

Append-only snapshot

Previous/current values, source, rule version, override reason, actor, and timestamp are preserved.

6

Project indicator

Circular progress, milestone/KPI context, previous/current comparison, and next evidence gap.

Valid inputs

  • Approved milestone acceptance conditions.
  • Approved KPI baseline, target, unit, direction, and measured value.
  • Required Evidence verified through the contract rule.
  • Authorized human confirmation or manual override with reason and audit.

Never used to calculate progress

  • Number of completed Tasks or total Task volume.
  • Update frequency or GitHub activity.
  • Commits, pull requests, files, or lines changed.
  • Employee effort, activity volume, or evaluation rating.

Fact-first, human-decided evaluation

Work records help both sides remember and discuss contribution. They do not calculate the person's rating. Employee and manager use the same approved anchors, assess independently, then compare.

Manager final decision
1

Cycle snapshot

Eligibility, rubric, locale, visibility mode, responsibilities, and criteria are frozen for the cycle.

2

Fact View

Source-supported facts and coverage gaps appear before employee interpretation.

3

Self-assessment

The employee selects a rating and writes interpretation; AI may help wording only afterward.

4

Manager assessment

The manager records an independent initial position before seeing the comparison.

5

Comparison & discussion

Differences are discussed using facts, interpretation, and manager judgment—not an AI recommendation.

6

Final human judgment

The manager records the final rating; changes from the initial position require a reason.

7

Acknowledge or reserve

The employee may acknowledge or preserve a reservation without changing the manager's decision.

8

Immutable history

Finalized snapshots and exports remain version-pinned, auditable, expiring, and revocable where defined.

×

AI output prohibited in evaluation

No suggested, predicted, or recommended performance rating; no employee rank; no productivity score; no rating derived from Project count, documentation readiness, GitHub volume, or activity frequency.

8

AI helps without taking control

Every production AI call passes through the AI Router. Feature modules never call a provider SDK directly. Model choice is based on task complexity, cost, privacy, and the approved route—not on a browser preference.

GPT-5.6 policy active

AI may

Connect authorized context, summarize sources, identify missing information, explain a suggestion, prepare an editable draft, and offer a safe next action.

AI does not

Confirm Evidence, activate a Progress Contract, change official Project progress, execute a protected command, recommend a rating, rank employees, or replace a manager's final human decision.

9

Technical architecture

A modular monolith keeps the user experience connected while protecting domain ownership. Modules communicate through public interfaces and approved query services, not direct cross-domain table reads.

Modular monolith
Experience

Next.js Command Brief web application, server-rendered protected routes, same-origin gateways, responsive EN/AR foundations, accessible primitives, and rollback flags.

API & domains

NestJS API with explicit modules for identity, Projects, Work Items, Documents, Updates/Evidence, Research, Evaluation, Coaching, Continuity, Operations, and Administration.

Authority

PostgreSQL stores authoritative state, versioned configuration, responsibility windows, immutable snapshots, idempotency keys, receipts, and audit history.

Identity

Keycloak/OIDC authenticates users. Server-side permission decisions use current role, scope, responsibility window, lifecycle state, and protected mode.

Async work

Redis and BullMQ carry bounded jobs, durable receipt wake-ups, retries, and replay. PostgreSQL remains authoritative when a queue is unavailable.

Private objects

MinIO stores private uploaded objects behind signed access. Type, size, ownership, and malware-scan gates are checked before official processing.

How one protected action moves

1

User intent

The browser submits the reviewed action and current version.

2

Authenticate

OIDC session becomes a server principal; inactive accounts fail closed.

3

Authorize

The owning module checks role, scope, responsibility, privacy mode, and current state.

4

Transact

Authoritative state, version transition, receipt, and audit are committed consistently.

5

Project

The UI reloads the authorized projection and handles stale or partial failure truthfully.

Authority, privacy, and history

Identity, role, Project responsibility, and evaluation authority are different. UI visibility never replaces server authorization, and deactivation never deletes protected history.

Role / context May do Must not do History rule
Employee

Manage assigned work, private Capture, own Updates/Evidence, Research, self-assessment, and acknowledgment.

Confirm another person's contribution or alter manager judgment.

Own confirmations and revisions remain attributable.

Project / Workstream owner

Coordinate scope, sources, contract review, criteria review, and authorized Project decisions.

Act as an employee's manager merely because of ownership.

Responsibility follows exact effective windows.

Manager

Resolve department operations, approve continuity actions, assess independently, and make the final rating decision.

Access private employee source context or receive AI-generated ratings.

Initial and final positions are separately preserved.

System administrator

Govern identity, configuration, AI routes, retention, audit, and operations.

Decide Project reassignment or employee performance ratings.

Sensitive operations require reason and audit.

Acting owner

Perform only the approved action set, for the exact Project/Workstream and time window.

Inherit broad manager or permanent owner authority.

Activation, expiry, return, and handover remain recorded.

Pilot feedback mode

Upward manager feedback is Identified. The authorized manager sees submitter identity, completion status, ratings, comments, and timestamps. The product must not claim anonymity.

Historical integrity

Active templates and closed evaluations are immutable. Criteria are versioned and never retroactive. Project, Evidence, responsibility, and evaluation history survives account deactivation.
