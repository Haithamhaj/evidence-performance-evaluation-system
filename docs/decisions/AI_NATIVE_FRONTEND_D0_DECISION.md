# AI-Native Frontend D0 Decision

**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Owner:** Product Owner  
**State:** `APPROVED`

## Decision

Adopt **Command Brief** as the Phase 0A visual and information-hierarchy direction for the AI-native
frontend. The approved default employee home is Today, ordered as:

1. Needs Your Decision.
2. Prepared for You.
3. Today.
4. Continue.
5. What Changed.

Keep Work, Projects, Research, and Evaluation as recognizable destinations. Manager and Admin remain
authorization-driven destinations. On mobile, preserve the compact bottom navigation and use focused
drawers/sheets for decisions and capture.

## Authority Granted

This positive D0 authorizes a separate **Phase 0B foundation plan** covering ADRs, exact Phase 1–2
engine handoffs, product tokens, primitive compatibility, Storybook/testing, import boundaries, the
Stable Shell, inspection contracts, route retirement, and Gate G0 preparation.

It does **not** authorize Phase 1 runtime implementation, production Today behavior, new business
rules, agent orchestration, AI rating output, or any change to protected product boundaries.

## Constraints Carried Forward

- AI prepares, connects, summarizes, and explains; it never assigns or recommends ratings.
- Project progress remains contract- and source-based and is never employee performance.
- Manual paths remain complete without Chat or AI.
- Smart output must expose source, why, freshness, consequence, and a safe recovery path.
- Arabic/RTL foundations continue; Arabic employee evaluation remains blocked by T016.
- Work Signals, Experience Workflow Events, and Product Telemetry remain separate.
- Production navigation follows authorization; the prototype role switch is not a production model.
- No code, assets, schema, dependency, or branding from the discarded experimental ClickUp branch may
  enter the product.

## Evidence and Limitations

The decision is based on direct Product Owner review of the selected live Arabic employee experience,
focused automated interaction coverage, design QA, capability reconciliation, and architecture
feasibility review. Human roles overlapped; no separate manager participant session occurred.

Before G0, run one distinct daily-employee session and one manager/Project-owner session. This is a
bounded validation follow-up, not permission to reopen the approved direction without a confirmed
P0/P1 issue or protected-rule conflict.
