# Internal Beta User Guide

## Start your day

Open Home for the overview: what needs your decision, what is due, confirmed Project progress, and
the next source-backed action. Project progress comes only from the approved Project contract or an
authorized owner confirmation. It is not your performance score.

## Work

Use Work for normal Tasks. Create a Task with a title and required Project, open its focused detail,
and move it through the available statuses. Board and Calendar are alternative views of the same
Tasks. Completing Tasks alone does not change Project progress.

When the assistant prepares one action, review the source, reason, freshness, and consequence. You
remain in control. Use `Helpful` or one improvement category to improve future suggestions; this
feedback is about the product and is not part of your evaluation.

## Share anything

Capture text, a link, voice, an image, code, or a file. The assistant proposes the likely Project and
meaning, then asks only for missing context. Nothing becomes an official Update, Evidence record, or
progress change until you review and confirm the relevant action.

## Sources and evidence

Google context stays private to you until you link or confirm it. GitHub is suggested evidence only.
If a connector is unavailable, use manual capture with a screenshot, code, link, or file. Confirmed
Evidence must describe the supported claim and your contribution context.

## Evaluation

Your Project, Tasks, Updates, and Evidence help present source-supported facts. They do not calculate
your rating. You complete your self-assessment, your manager makes the final human assessment, and AI
must not recommend a rating.

## If something fails

- Retry once when the product presents a retry action.
- Use the visible manual fallback if an external provider is unavailable.
- Sign in again when the session has expired.
- Report a suggestion using the fixed feedback choices; do not paste private content into support
  messages.

For access, privacy, or incorrect-person data, stop using the affected screen and contact the internal
pilot support owner immediately.
